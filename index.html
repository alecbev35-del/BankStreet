Downtown Bristol, TN

Location

Why we chose this location

Bristol sits at the crossroads of music history and mountain country — the official Birthplace of Country Music, with State Street running right down the Tennessee–Virginia line. Our quarters are a short walk from the best of downtown: restaurants, live venues, the Birthplace of Country Music Museum, and the iconic Bristol sign.

State Street

Steps from restaurants, coffee, and live music. The heartbeat of downtown is right outside the door.

Music Heritage

The Birthplace of Country Music Museum and a year-round calendar of performances sit within a few blocks.

Mountain Access

South Holston Lake, the Blue Ridge, and Appalachian hikes are a short drive. City by night, mountains by day.

Bristol Motor Speedway

Race weekends are a short drive from our front door. Stay close to the action without the traffic.
