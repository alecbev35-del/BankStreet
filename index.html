Discover Bristol

A local guide to eating, drinking, and exploring.

From rooftop bars and the Country Music Museum to swimming holes and underground caves — here's everything we send our guests to. Hand-picked, regularly updated.

Things to Do — In Town

Top Pick

Birthplace of Country Music Museum

101 Country Music Way, Bristol VA

Interactive exhibits on the 1927 Bristol Sessions — the recordings that launched country music. A live radio station broadcasts from inside. Closed Mondays.

Paramount Bristol

518 State St, Bristol TN

Restored 1930s theater. Concerts, comedy, classic films.

Bristol Motor Speedway

Bristol TN

Iconic short-track NASCAR speedway. Weekday tours. Speedway in Lights at Christmas is a big draw.

Hard Rock Hotel & Casino Bristol

500 Gate City Hwy, Bristol VA

The region's newest landmark. 1,300+ slots, full table games, sportsbook, and a multi-restaurant complex. Open 24/7.

Lakes & Boating

Top Pick

South Holston Lake

~20 min south

The local go-to lake. Crystal-clear Cherokee National Forest reservoir. Boat & jet-ski rentals, fishing, swimming, and lakeside dining. The easiest day trip from your door.

Watauga Lake

~45 min east

Sapphire mountain lake — quieter and more scenic than South Holston. Ideal for kayaking, pontoon days, and lakeside picnics.

Boone Lake

~25 min south

The closer-in option. Marinas, family-friendly coves, and reliable bass fishing.

Cherokee National Forest

~30 min

Half a million acres of mountains, rivers, and lake access. Tube floats, hiking, fly-fishing the Watauga and South Holston tailwaters.

Parks, Trails & Outdoors

Top Pick

Mendota Trail

3460 Island Rd, Bristol VA

Rails-to-trails path running 12.5 miles to Mendota. Easy grade with a dramatic swinging bridge mid-trail. Walk a section or bike the full out-and-back.

Steele Creek Park

4 Little Ln, Bristol TN

Huge city park — lake, hiking, disc golf, splash pad, kid train ride. $2 to drive in.

Steele Creek Nature Center

80 Lakeshore Dr, Bristol TN

Inside Steele Creek. Interactive wildlife exhibits. Ask to meet Big Snappy the snapping turtle.

Rooster Front at Steele Creek

1160 Vance Dr, Bristol TN

Other Steele Creek entrance — over a dozen trails, falls, basketball court, skate area.

Holston River Weir Dam

376 Holston View Dam Rd, Bristol TN

Free riverside trails. 1.5 miles paved / gravel. Eagles, picnic spots. Easy walk.

Observation Knob Park

337 Knob Park Rd, Bristol TN

Campground + day-use park on South Holston Lake. Lake views, trails, picnicking.

Day Trips

Top Pick

Natural Tunnel State Park

Duffield VA · ~45 min west

Massive natural railway tunnel cut through a mountain — a true bucket-list landmark. Chairlift access down to the floor, hiking trails, and camping. Worth the drive.

Abingdon, VA

~25 min northeast

Barter Theatre · 127 W Main St — longest-running pro theater in the US (since 1933). Year-round shows.
Abingdon Muster Grounds — Revolutionary War historical site. Walkable grounds, small museum.
Sweetbay Brewing — best beer stop in Abingdon.
Pair with the Virginia Creeper Trail for a full day.

Blue Hole Falls

Elizabethton TN · ~35 min

Series of waterfalls + swimming holes. Short but steep hike down. Local summer hangout.
